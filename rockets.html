Welcome to the U10 Rockets Team Page

The U10 Rockets are part of our girls U10 age group, focused on fun, teamwork, and football skills.

Training takes place on Fridays at 6:00pm, and league matches are played in the NEHYL on Sundays. All abilities are welcome!

We are currently looking for new players! If your daughter is interested in joining the U10 Rockets, please get in touch with us at secretary@hartyouthfc.uk.
